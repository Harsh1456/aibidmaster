Scope & Requirements

Scope of Work

The project involves expanding the existing 4-lane highway to 6 lanes along a 3.5 mile stretch. Work includes excavation, grading, asphalt paving, drainage improvements, and installation of new signage and road markings. The project must be completed with minimal disruption to existing traffic flow.

Special Requirements

All work must be completed between 9 PM and 5 AM to minimize traffic disruption. Contractor must provide traffic control plans approved by city DOT. Environmental mitigation measures required near wetland areas.
